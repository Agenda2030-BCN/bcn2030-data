**Note**

Residential and ‘tertiary’ uses (administrative, commercial, or service buildings) are included. Certification is not mandatory, except for new buildings and major renovation projects. In the residential sector, however, the energy efficiency certificate is required if the property is to be sold or rented out. The certificate may also be requested voluntarily or to obtain certain grants
